Consulted 14 November 2026.

1. Espressif, Arduino-ESP32 ADC API: analogReadMilliVolts, resolution, and attenuation ranges. https://docs.espressif.com/projects/arduino-esp32/en/latest/api/adc.html
2. Espressif, ESP32 series datasheet: verify pin electrical limits and classic ESP32 pin functions. https://www.espressif.com/sites/default/files/documentation/esp32_datasheet_en.pdf
3. Analog Devices, LTC3588-1 product documentation: integrated rectifier/buck harvesting architecture. https://www.analog.com/en/products/ltc3588-1.html
4. Analog Devices, LTC3588-1 datasheet: operating limits, external components, and application circuits. https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/35881fc.pdf
5. PlatformIO, Espressif ESP32 Dev Module board configuration. https://docs.platformio.org/en/latest/boards/espressif32/esp32dev.html

These sources inform the proposed architecture; they do not verify StepCharge's mechanical design or demonstrate a particular energy output.
